# L3 handoff: the 3-bet-pot leak

Branch `tier4/l3` on top of `origin/tier4/int` (code = int's 59ddb7d). Laptop, nproc 14, Intel Core Ultra 5 225H, lock rules.
Logs: `logs/l3/` (base/F/R10/R15 screens 40 pairs at seeds 75000+, KEEP kb_*/kr_* 50 pairs at 75100+, guard 75200+, timing.txt).

**Verdict: formal KEEP on both parts, but within noise. Not a proven gain. `x_3b_ref` stays OFF (default 0).**

## Where the chips go (tier3x on int, locks off, 40 pairs, new `--report lines`)
"They re-raise our raise, we continue" pots (`3bet_opp`), chips/hand ± SE (per pot); their re-raise rate in brackets:
conv_opp3 -0.264 ± 0.077 (-7.7, 8 %) · trapper -0.173 ± 0.042 (-27.7, 1.6 %) · minraise -0.120 ± 0.073 (-2.2, 13 %) ·
exploiter -0.063 ± 0.058 (-2.6, 6 %) · lag -0.004 ± 0.215 (-0.0, 25 %). Folding preflop costs ~-5/pot.
Cause: preflop, not postflop play. We continue ~90 % of our raises vs a re-raise against every bot. Tier 3's table keeps the top 60 %
of all hands (~86 % of opens), and `eq_floor` (Tier 2's pot-odds rule) reads equity vs a *random* hand, which 73 % of all hands
pass at a 3-bet's price (only 22 % vs the top-30 % table). Their limp-raise of our BB raise goes to Tier 2 (not a table spot), same problem.

## Fixes tried (screen: base vs arm, 40 pairs, locks off, sum over bots)
- F `x_3b_floor` (floor vs top-30 % table, rate-blended): sum -0.75 ± 0.63, lag -0.91 ± 0.47, minraise -0.16. DROP (code removed).
- K `x_3b_k` (cap continue at k x rate): not run (it carried F's floor); code removed.
- **R `x_3b_ref` (kept as the flag):** lam = (ref - their measured 3-bet rate)/ref in [0,1]; moves the button's continue share toward
  `x_3b_min` 0.25 and the floor's equity (Tier 3 table spot and Tier 2's `choose`) toward the top-30 % share. Rate >= ref: Tier 3 exactly.
  Screen R10 (ref 0.10): +0.13 ± 0.44. R15: +0.02 ± 0.43. The 3-bet line vs trapper improves (-0.173 -> -0.008..-0.144), the totals don't move.

## KEEP, R10 vs tier3x, fresh seeds 75100+, 50 pairs, back to back
- Locks off chips: conv_opp3 +0.17 ± 0.17, exploiter -0.03 ± 0.16, lag +0.38 ± 0.41, minraise -0.11 ± 0.17, trapper +0.15 ± 0.21;
  **sum +0.56 ± 0.54 (z +1.0)**, nobody down > 2 SE: KEEP. The move never fires vs lag (rate 25 %), so lag's +0.38 is pure noise

- Locks on score: overall 0.802 -> 0.800, -0.002 ± 0.024: KEEP-score (trapper 0.97 -> 0.93 ± 0.031).
- Simple-bot guard (20 pairs, locks on): all six 1.000.
- Timing (400 hands, seed 1): trapper 5.37 s vs Tier 3 5.35 s (x1.00), conv_opp3 5.22 vs 5.39 (x0.97); slow timing tests 10 passed.
- Fast suite 1350 passed, 2 skipped.

## Decision for the manager
The leak is real but small: at most ~+0.17/hand vs trapper and ~+0.1 vs conv_opp3 if those pots broke even. That is below what
50 pairs resolve (SE of a difference ~0.2/bot). Both seed blocks are positive but not significant. Turning it on is safe on time, the guard
and the score, but there's no evidence of a gain. My call: leave it OFF unless a 200-pair sequential run says otherwise.
